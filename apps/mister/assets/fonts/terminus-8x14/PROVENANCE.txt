The generated MMBF resources expand every native 8x14 BDF pixel to a 2x2
composition block. CRT240's exact 2:1 vertical reduction then preserves each
source pixel as a 2x1 framebuffer pixel, which has square physical geometry on
the 4:3 640x240 output.
