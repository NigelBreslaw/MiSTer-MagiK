Yesterday 10 and Xerxes 10

MiSTer MagiK uses monochrome glyph subsets generated from the commercially
licensed Yesterday 10 and Xerxes 10 font software. The source fonts and
standalone generated font resources are private licensed assets and are not
distributed as part of the public source repository.

The commercial licences are held by the MiSTer MagiK distributor. All rights
in the original font software remain with their respective owner.
