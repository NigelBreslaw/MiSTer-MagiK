MiSTer MagiK card artwork

Rendered and composed for MiSTer MagiK. Original model credits and license
links follow. Models were repositioned, scaled, and lit for card artwork;
materials were adjusted as noted. Baked studio floors, background sweeps and
compositor bloom were removed for the black-background card renders.
Only rendered images ship with the app.
