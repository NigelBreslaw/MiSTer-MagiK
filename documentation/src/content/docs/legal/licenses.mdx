GPL-3.0-or-later, including the first-party scanout kernel module. Every
distribution ZIP carries the applicable GPL text and the complete third-party
inventory in `licenses/RUST-LIBRARIES.txt`.

The scanout module records its GPL-3.0-or-later source license separately from
the Linux loader's compatibility classification. The shipped FPGA latch also
includes Menu_MiSTer-derived material under its upstream GPL-3.0 terms; each
release ZIP names the exact corresponding source revisions.

The launcher uses Slint under its GPL-3.0-only option. Its applicable GPL text
is the MiSTer MagiK GPL below. The Rust inventory covers normal runtime
dependencies for the ARM launcher build and is intentionally kept out of the
launcher UI and this page.
