MiSTer MagiK is GPL-3.0-or-later. The launcher’s Licenses screen presents the
three directly bundled license texts below. Every distribution ZIP also carries
the complete third-party inventory in `licenses/RUST-LIBRARIES.txt`.

The launcher uses Slint under its GPL-3.0-only option. Its applicable GPL text
is the MiSTer MagiK GPL below. The Rust inventory covers normal runtime
dependencies for the ARM launcher build and is intentionally kept out of the
launcher UI and this page.
